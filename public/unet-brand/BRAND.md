# Unet Systems: logo and colour rules

Tagline: **Enterprise technology for modern business transformation.**
Personality: precise, trustworthy, calm, engineered.

## Logo
- Symbol: a cube split into three separated faces, meaning separate parts assembled into one system. The top face is the only cyan element; the two side faces are ink.
- Wordmark: "Unet" in Onest Bold, "Systems" in Onest Regular. Already outlined in the SVGs; never retype it.
- Lockups: horizontal (default for navbars and footers), stacked, symbol only.

## Colours
| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Ink | `#0D0D0F` | Text, logo on light backgrounds, dark background |
| Cyan (on dark) | `#21D2ED` | Accent on dark backgrounds |
| Cyan (on light) | `#0B9CB5` | Accent on light backgrounds |
| Light background | `#F6F7F9` | Light surfaces, logo on dark backgrounds |

Use cyan sparingly, as a highlight, not a fill.

## Backgrounds
- Light background: `unet-*-light.svg`. Dark background: `unet-*-dark.svg`.
- One colour (print, stamps, single-ink): `unet-*-black.svg` or `unet-*-white.svg`.
- On photos or busy colours, use black or white, whichever has more contrast.

## Size and spacing
- Clear space: keep at least half the symbol's height free on all sides.
- Minimum sizes: symbol alone 16px; horizontal lockup 20px tall; stacked lockup 48px tall.
- Navbar: horizontal lockup, about 24px tall, left-aligned.

## Don't
- Don't recolour the faces, add gradients, shadows or outlines, or rotate or stretch the logo.
- Don't make more than the top face cyan.
- Don't place the light-background logo on dark backgrounds, or the reverse.
- Don't redraw or retype the wordmark; use the supplied SVGs.
